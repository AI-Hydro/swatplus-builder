# Prospective multi-basin evaluation v1 (PE1)

Status: **protocol fixed before any PE1 run.** This file, the basin list
`basins/prospective_eval_v1_basins.json`, and its pre-flight record
`basins/prospective_eval_v1_basins.preflight.json` are committed before the
first workflow is launched. The commit that adds them is the prospective
record. This is a prospectively specified design recorded in version control;
it is not a registration with an external registry.

## 1. Question

The manuscript's focused evidence consists of two basins chosen to show
contrasting outcomes, and both were used during software development. PE1 asks:

> When the current package runs the focused-case workflow, unchanged, on
> basins that it has never seen and that were selected by a documented rule,
> how do the evidence gates and claim decisions behave?

PE1 evaluates the governance mechanism at scale. It is not a hydrologic
benchmark, an optimiser comparison, or a test of agent behaviour.

## 2. Basin selection (fixed)

1. Population: `basins/reference_pool_v1.json`, generated by
   `scripts/basin_inclusion_protocol.py` (docs/BASIN_INCLUSION_PROTOCOL.md).
   It contains active USGS stream gauges in CONUS with daily discharge,
   drainage area between 50 and 3,000 mi², and no prior use anywhere in this
   repository's development or testing history.
2. Group: `development` only. The `held_out_final_assessment` group is not
   touched by PE1 and stays reserved.
3. Feasibility cap: drainage area ≤ 600 km². Full-overlay runs scale with
   HRU count. The focused cases took 39 min (111 km²) and 39 h (2,193 km²)
   of wall-clock time. The cap bounds cost on one laptop. It also narrows
   scope: PE1 says nothing about basins above 600 km².
4. Exclusion: basins with a `usgs_<id>` run directory already under `runs/`.
5. Ordering: SHA-256 of `swat-s1-pe1-v1:<usgs_id>`, ascending.
6. Discharge pre-flight: in that order, each candidate's NWIS daily
   discharge for 2010-01-01 to 2019-12-31 is fetched with the pipeline's own
   `fetch_usgs_daily_q`. A candidate is kept if at least 95% of days are
   present. Selection stops at 12 kept basins.

Outcome of selection: 13 candidates checked, 1 rejected (03576500, coverage
0.8105), 12 kept. Every check is recorded in the pre-flight file.

## 3. Workflow (fixed, identical to the focused cases)

`swat workflow run` with: `--model-family full`, `--start 2010-01-01`,
`--end 2019-12-31`, `--warmup-years 3` (simulation begins 2007),
`--calibrate`, `--claim-tier research_grade`, `--hru-mode full_overlay`,
`--min-hru-fraction 0.001`, `--contract-status accepted`,
`--accepted-by policy`. This document is the policy that accepts the
contract. The default chronological 60/40 split gives calibration scoring on
2010–2015 and withheld validation on 2016–2019, as in the focused cases.

Execution: `scripts/decision_data_batch.py --workers 2`, with
`--default-sensitivity-workers 2 --default-anchor-workers 2` (the focused
cases used 4 and 4). Worker counts change how independent evaluations are
scheduled, not which evaluations run or how they are scored. Per-basin
timeout: 6 h. A timeout is recorded as an outcome.

Software: the commit that adds this file. It differs from the focused cases'
0.7.14 revision (`90eeec95df7e`) in ways that are recorded in CHANGELOG
[Unreleased]. The ones that bear on PE1 are:

- The calibration phase score uses the scale-aware `log_kge_v2` in place of
  the fixed-epsilon `log_kge`.
- GridMET temperature inversions of at most 1.0 K on at most 0.2% of days are
  repaired and disclosed instead of failing the basin.
- The tamper-evident event and decision ledgers are now written.

PE1 is therefore a current-version evaluation. It is not a replication of
the 0.7.14 focused runs.

## 4. Pre-specified outcomes

All are descriptive. With n = 12, no hypothesis test or population rate is
inferred.

- O1. Flow of basins through the stages: selected, run completed, workflow
  success, and effective tier. Every basin appears in the denominator.
- O2. Distribution of effective claim tiers.
- O3. Frequency of each failed gate and each blocker class.
- O4. Metric change against claim promotion. For each basin that reached
  locked verification: the change in NSE and KGE from benchmark to
  verification, and whether the workflow was promoted. The mechanism predicts
  that improvement alone does not determine promotion. Any basin that
  improved and was not promoted is evidence for that. A promoted basin that
  did not improve would be a governance failure, and would be reported as one.
- O5. Withheld 2016–2019 transfer: pass/fail and metrics per basin.
- O6. Cost: wall-clock time and engine candidate evaluations per basin.
- O7. Disclosed weather repairs and imputations per basin.
- O8. Ledger verification (`swat audit verify`) per basin.

## 5. Rules

- No gate thresholds are changed for PE1, before or after results are seen.
- No basin is dropped after it has been launched. Failures are outcomes.
- One rerun is allowed only for infrastructure failure (network or provider
  outage, machine interruption), and only if the evidence shows the cause.
  Every rerun is logged in §6 with its reason. Scientific failures are never
  rerun.
- If a software defect is found during PE1, it is logged in §6 and fixed in a
  separate commit. Affected basins are reported both as originally observed
  and, if rerun, as a clearly labelled post-fix result.
