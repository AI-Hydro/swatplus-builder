## Recommendation

Keep governed DDS as the reference. First repair evaluation accounting and objective/cache semantics; then test a **single-region constrained Gaussian-process optimizer** through a shared exact-engine evaluator. Test RBF-DYCORS as a second surrogate comparator when its initial design has sufficient rank. Parallel independent DDS starts are a hardware experiment, not an assumed saving in evaluations. Production defaults and historical PE1 evidence remain unchanged.

The proposal combines three independent audits and an adversarial review:

## What the evidence supports

Calibration occupied 39,071 of 47,044.209 summed PE1 workflow seconds (83.05%). These overlapping basin intervals are not batch elapsed time or DDS-only runtime. Screening constitutes 300 of 558 retained screen/search traces (53.8% of trace cardinality, not runtime). The reported 593 candidate evaluations is an estimator: 320 assumed screen bounds plus 273 primary phase events. It is not an exact count of solver launches. Failures, cache hits, duplicate vectors and verification cannot be reconstructed completely from these counts.

A recorded July experiment found four-worker batch DDS at 584.70 seconds versus serial at 583.94 seconds. Input-only staging already delivered a recorded 3.9% reduction and is already implemented. New gains must be measured rather than credited to these existing features. Thirty-two parameter hashes overlap between screening and search; actual simulation-context compatibility must be checked before any reuse or saving claim.

The offline probe tests six cases: positive and intermittent synthetic discharge, plus benchmark/verification series from two previously inspected development basins, restricted to 2010–2015. Across four unit factors, raw KGE, square-root NSE and observed-mean-offset log NSE remain invariant within `1e-10`. Eight invalid transformed-metric cases are rejected. In the seven-value example, relative log-KGE changes from −4.86735 to +0.92166 when units are multiplied by 1,000. This is a formula defect, not evidence that a different optimizer will improve hydrology. Neither tested transformed NSE is adopted as a production objective by this report.

## Ranked engineering work

| Order | Change | Reason and acceptance evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Version the scientific objective; explicit finite/zero/missing rules | A faster search must not optimize unit-sensitive or silently missing terms. Keep historical metric versions replayable; separate objective experiments from optimizer comparisons. |
| 2 | Exact request, invocation and budget ledger; stage timings | Count screening, anchors, seeds, failures, cache hits and verification separately. Log secondary seeds. Small budgets must fail preflight rather than overrun silently. |
| 3 | Semantic cache identity and atomic single-flight publication | Bind engine/input/calendar/observation/parameter/metric/gate identities; concurrent identical requests launch once. Final locked verification remains fresh. |
| 4 | Parse outputs once; exact-compatible simulation reuse | Separate immutable raw output identity from rescoring identity. Preserve required routing/process evidence. Demonstrate output equality before measuring savings. |
| 5 | Optional constrained GP ask/tell strategy | Model finite utility and observed continuous constraint residuals separately. Errors are typed statuses, not fabricated extreme objective values. Surrogates propose; SWAT+ decides. |
| 6 | Resource-controlled parallel and fidelity trials | Control total workers across basins and engine threads. Shorter periods/coarser models need paired fidelity validation and full-fidelity confirmation. |

ET/P thresholds, filter-derived BFI and the closure proxy require scientific interpretation. The implemented closure omits explicit storage change. Preserve existing claim policy while testing any revised basin-context policy separately; increased acceptance alone does not establish improved physical validity. Endpoint screening can miss interior, interaction and low-flow effects; an inactive screen result must not become a universal identifiability claim.

The existing `autoresearch/surrogate.py` bootstrap linear ensemble is not a constrained GP optimizer. Its raw-parameter, unregularized fits, rank-insufficient defaults and uncalibrated ensemble spread need separate hardening. Reuse typed artifacts where appropriate; do not route predicted scores into scientific promotion.

## Proposed experiment, to freeze before engine runs

1. Choose three already exposed development basins spanning small/large simulation cost and contrasting runoff regimes. Freeze their identities and artifact hashes before any optimizer pilot. PE1 and manuscript-inspected basins cannot be described as untouched test cases.
2. Freeze one objective policy and process policy, normalized parameters, bounds, simulation and warm-up dates, observation alignment, engine/source hashes and hardware. Select transformed metrics/weights on development data only. Compare objective policies in a separate labeled experiment.
3. Implement serial paired arms: repaired-objective DDS, constrained GP with a conservative small shared design, and RBF-DYCORS only when adequately spanning data exist. Share compatible initial observations and charge their acquisition cost to every cold-start arm. Existing archived observations form a separately labeled warm-start experiment, with prior cost disclosed.
4. Pilot **60 and 120 total attempted engine calls per basin/seed**, with three seeds; preflight includes mandatory screening, initialization and final verification. A 30-call cold-start arm is infeasible when its required screen alone needs 31 successful traces; do not invent room by hiding screen or verification cost. At 14 dimensions the default cubic/linear-tail RBF requires at least 15 spanning observations. Check rank, duplicates and actual residual budget before launching. Pilot size and budgets are proposals, not approved executed experiments.
5. A shared budget service grants tokens before launch, records request/cache/invocation status, reserves final independent verification, and persists crash/cancel evidence. Report attempted calls, successful calls, cached requests and total elapsed/CPU time separately. The optimizer returns its best actually simulated admissible point; unavailable admissibility is not passing.
6. Measure training-admissible skill versus total calls/time, invalid-run fraction and paired basin/seed differences. Measure time to first **training-admissible** candidate. Independently verify the final selected candidate and assess the sealed temporal period once after all development decisions are frozen. Repeated held-out checks cannot be an optimizer stopping rule. For a larger confirmatory experiment, predeclare new untouched basins and sufficient replicates before viewing results.
7. Assess raw metric components, volume bias, low-flow/zero-flow behavior, recession and peaks, and evaluable process evidence. At fixed hardware compare elapsed time as well as evaluation budgets; a reduction in calls does not automatically establish a wall-clock saving. Predeclare quality tolerances and success targets after the feasibility pilot, before the confirmatory comparison.
8. Add 2–4-worker scheduling only after serial evaluation savings are known. Test independently bounded DDS starts separately; report CPU cost and completion-order effects. Leave multifidelity and donor transfer for later experiments.

## Contribution and precedent

Bayesian SWAT+ calibration already has a direct precedent ([BOA, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.envsoft.2024.106191)); parallel DDS and surrogate watershed optimization also have precedents. The defensible research hypothesis is **budget allocation that reduces the cost of independently verified admissible calibration while preserving objective, process and provenance evidence across basins**. Adaptive parameter groups, compatible simulation reuse and failure-aware proposals could contribute if controlled ablations show a benefit over strong references. No novelty or speedup is established yet.

The constrained proposal is informed by [SCBO](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v130/eriksson21a.html), but generic large-budget trust-region benchmarks do not establish a win at our budget. [Santos et al.](https://hess.copernicus.org/articles/22/4583/2018/) motivates avoiding log-KGE; [Wagener et al.](https://hess.copernicus.org/articles/30/4867/2026/) supports purpose-specific objective evaluation rather than a universal metric. Detailed primary-source access limits are recorded in the individual reports.
